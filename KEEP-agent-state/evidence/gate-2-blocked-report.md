## Blocking gaps

- Generic Semantic Model and Semantic IR schema. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** the Semantic IR's semantic role and generic-kernel boundary have been established by human authority. It is a normalized, provenance-preserving intermediate representation derived from the approved Doctrine Semantic Model. The kernel represents semantic concepts, relationships, invariants/constraints, lifecycle transitions, derivations, provenance, identity, version, scope, meaning, and applicable authority/acceptance context. Domain concepts are represented through the generic kernel rather than domain-specific IR types. It is not semantic authority, a ratification mechanism, or an implementation model. Exact TypeScript structure, field cardinality, serialization, normalization, ordering, and deterministic identity remain unresolved and are not implied by this decision.
- Canonicalization and deterministic identity mechanics. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** semantically equivalent IR states shall produce equivalent canonical representations independent of filesystem, incidental ordering, execution, language, and serialization details. Semantic ordering, variance-eliminating normalization, canonical-representation encoding, and identity derived from canonical semantic content are required; unresolved ambiguity must remain explicit and block canonicalization. Concrete formats, algorithms, and hash mechanisms remain implementation decisions.
- Authority, acceptance, provenance, uncertainty, and contradiction schemas. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** these remain distinct concepts with the approved meanings and non-collapse constraints; conflict precedence and resolution are deferred to Gap 10. Exact TypeScript structure, cardinality, serialization, and other implementation details remain unresolved.
- Lifecycle and acceptance transition semantics. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** lifecycle and acceptance remain distinct dimensions; ratification, applicability, supersession, rejection, and retirement are not collapsed or inferred. Transitions must be explicit, attributable, scoped, and provenance-preserving; acceptance requires an applicable authority decision. Exact enumerations and artifact-specific transition matrices remain unresolved.
- Semantic Version and compatibility semantics. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** version identifies semantic evolution relative to Semantic Identity and Scope; compatibility is semantic, contextual, and not inferred from version numbers. Version changes do not establish authority, acceptance, applicability, or supersession. Concrete versioning, compatibility comparison, representation, classification, and TypeScript algorithms remain unresolved.
- Runtime/SDK dependency relationship. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** Runtime and SDK are independent realizations of the applicable Semantic Contract, which is their shared semantic authority. Neither is the semantic source of the other; implementation dependencies are realization concerns. They remain independently versioned and identifiable relative to the applicable contract, with distinct compatibility relationships. Concrete API/package dependency structure and versioning mechanics remain unresolved.
- Runtime Contract identity, lifecycle, applicability, and ratification. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** Runtime Contracts are Guvna-owned semantic artifacts; Candidate, Ratified, and Applicable states remain distinct, with human authority required for Guvna Core ratification and defined conditions required for applicability. Runtime and SDK consume or conform to Applicable Runtime Contracts and cannot establish their authority or applicability. Concrete schema, enumerations, applicability algorithm, ratification record, and TypeScript implementation remain unresolved.
- Projection compilation authority, validation, applicability, and stale/incompatible projection handling. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** compilation operates under the applicable Guvna-owned Projection Contract and remains subordinate to Repository Authority for repository-specific meaning. Conformance, applicability, provenance, identity, compatibility, staleness, invalidity, and incompatibility remain distinct; non-conformant projections cannot be consumed as conformant. Concrete schemas and algorithms remain unresolved.
- Runtime's authoritative repository input boundary. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** accepted Repository Knowledge is the authoritative semantic basis; required Governance Projections must be applicable, conformant, compatible, and traceable to it. Runtime cannot select, repair, revise, or replace authoritative repository meaning and must apply contract-defined validation or fail-closed outcomes for disagreement or invalid inputs. Concrete input, source-selection, loading, caching, and TypeScript mechanics remain unresolved.
- Conflict precedence among accepted doctrine sources. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** established semantic authority governs, preserving Constitutional Doctrine > Canonical Models > Architectural Doctrine > downstream realization ordering. Equal-authority conflicts without an established precedence relationship remain explicit and block deterministic compilation. Apparent conflict is not automatically contradiction; equivalence requires governing rules. All conflicting-source provenance and attributable resolution decisions must be preserved. Concrete precedence structures and algorithms remain unresolved.
- Authority and delegation model. **SEMANTICALLY RESOLVED:** authority is attributable to an Authority Identity; delegation explicitly identifies principals, delegated identity, scope, capabilities, conditions, governing authority, and provenance. Delegation is contained by the delegator's authority and does not imply ratification authority or cross ownership boundaries. Capability, scope, conditions, provenance, lifecycle, and further-delegation constraints remain explicit. Concrete schemas and mechanics remain unresolved.
